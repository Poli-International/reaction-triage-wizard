## What is Tattoo & Piercing Reaction Triage Wizard?

The Tattoo & Piercing Reaction Triage Wizard is a free, interactive decision-tree tool that helps identify whether a tattoo or piercing reaction is normal healing, simple irritation, a possible infection, or a medical emergency. The tool presents a series of targeted questions based on the type of procedure, timing since the procedure, and specific symptoms. Each answer leads to more refined questions until the tool can provide a clear verdict with specific recommended actions.

The wizard covers two main procedure types (body piercing and tattoo) and evaluates symptoms across three timeframes for each: fresh, healing, and healed. It uses clinical triage logic to distinguish between normal healing processes (like lymph fluid, peeling, and mild itching) and concerning symptoms (like spreading redness, coloured discharge, fever, or systemic illness). Results include a severity level, explanation of what the symptoms mean, and a numbered list of recommended actions.

## Who Should Use This

### Tattoo and Piercing Clients

## How to Use

### Step 1: Start the Wizard
The tool loads automatically when you visit the page. You will see the first question: "What type of procedure did you have?"

### Step 2: Select Your Procedure Type
Click either "Body piercing" or "Tattoo" based on your procedure. Each option has a subtitle to help you choose correctly.

### Step 3: Indicate the Timing
Select how long ago the procedure was done. Options vary by procedure type:
- For piercings: Less than 1 week, 1 week - 8 weeks, More than 8 weeks
- For tattoos: Less than 2 weeks, 2 weeks - 3 months, More than 3 months

### Step 4: Describe Your Main Symptom
Choose the symptom that best matches what you are experiencing. Each option includes an icon and a subtitle with additional context.

### Step 5: Answer Follow-Up Questions
Based on your previous answers, the tool will ask 1-3 additional questions to narrow down the diagnosis. These questions cover:
- Redness extent and spread
- Discharge characteristics
- Fever or systemic symptoms
- Timing of bump appearance
- Jewellery material
- Ink colour affected

### Step 6: Review Your Results
After completing the questions, you will see a result card with:
- A verdict (Normal healing, Irritation, See your piercer, See a doctor, or Seek urgent medical care)
- An explanation of what your symptoms mean
- A numbered list of recommended actions
- Jewellery recommendations if applicable

### Step 7: Restart if Needed
Click "Start over - assess a different symptom" to begin a new assessment. Use the "Back" button to return to previous questions if you made a mistake.

**Inputs:**
- Procedure type: Body piercing
- Timing: 1 week - 8 weeks
- Main symptom: Small raised bump next to the hole

**Output Actions:**
- Strip back to saline only
- Reduce cleaning to once daily

**Inputs:**
- Procedure type: Tattoo
- Timing: Less than 2 weeks
- Main symptom: Redness and mild swelling - warm to touch
- Redness spread: Staying within or just around the tattooed area

**Result:** Normal healing

**Output Actions:**
- Continue twice-daily sterile saline rinse
- Do not touch with unwashed hands
- Expect mild tenderness and occasional itching
- Itching during healing is often a sign of cell proliferation

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Is this tool a replacement for seeing a doctor?
No. This tool provides educational guidance only. If you have fever, rapidly spreading redness, significant swelling, or feel unwell after a procedure, seek medical attention immediately.

### Q2: How accurate is the triage wizard?
The tool uses standard clinical triage logic based on common healing patterns and infection signs. However, individual cases vary. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical decisions.

### Q3: Can I use this for an old piercing that suddenly has problems?
Yes. The tool includes a "More than 8 weeks" option for piercings and a "More than 3 months" option for tattoos, covering healed sites that develop new symptoms.

### Q4: What if my symptoms don't match any of the options?
Choose the option that most closely matches your symptoms. If you are unsure, or if your symptoms are severe, err on the side of caution and seek medical attention.

### Q5: Should I remove my jewellery if the tool says "See a doctor"?
No. The tool explicitly advises not to remove jewellery without medical advice, as removing it while infected can trap bacteria inside the closing channel.

### Q6: What does "BioFlex®" mean in the results?
BioFlex® is a flexible polymer jewellery material that reduces irritation for movement-stressed placements and clients with metal sensitivities. The tool mentions it when irritation is likely related to jewellery material or movement.

### Q7: Can I go back to change an answer?
Yes. Each screen has a "Back" button that returns you to the previous question. You can also restart entirely with the "Start over" button.

### Q8: How long does the assessment take?
Most assessments take 2-4 questions and less than 2 minutes to complete, depending on the complexity of your symptoms.

### Q9: What if I have both a tattoo and piercing reaction?
The tool assesses one procedure at a time. Complete the assessment for your most concerning symptom first, then restart to assess the other.

### Q10: Does the tool store my answers?
No. The tool runs entirely in your browser. No answers are saved, transmitted, or stored anywhere.
